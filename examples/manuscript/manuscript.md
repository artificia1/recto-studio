# The Lantern at Low Tide

Mara found the lantern where the stone steps disappeared into the harbor. Its glass was cold, but the wick still smelled of smoke. She set it on the harbor wall and watched the tide turn.

The boats had all come in before the rain. Only the night ferryman still worked the eastern crossing, where the channel was deepest.[^harbor]

## A note on the map

The old map marked the far landing with a square. Mara drew a circle around it in pencil, then folded the map so the ink would stay dry.

[^harbor]: The eastern crossing is a fictional place.
